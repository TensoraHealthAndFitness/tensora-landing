Why Tensora

A log tells you what you lifted. Tensora tells you what it built.

Most workout logs

  • Leave you to decide what to train today
  • Show rows of numbers, not muscles
  • Lose the session when the app closes
  • Never tell you which muscle is falling behind

Tensora

  • Builds your week from your body, goal and schedule
  • Maps every set onto a 2D and 3D body
  • Saves the workout as you go and syncs when online
  • Shows recovery and balance, so you know where to aim

How it works

From first open to first map in three steps

1

Tell it about you

Height, weight, your goal and how often you can train. Four short steps, no account to create.

2

Train today’s plan

Open Tensora, start the session and log each set in Grind with a rest timer running for you.

3

Watch the map fill in

Physique and Pivot turn your sets into a muscle map, recovery estimates, charts and streaks.
